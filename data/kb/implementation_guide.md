## Standard delivery pattern

A typical Enterprise Cloud implementation lasts six to eight weeks after prerequisites are complete. This range is a planning baseline, not a guaranteed completion date. The elapsed schedule begins only after scope, access, staffing, and technical prerequisites are confirmed.

The standard phases are kickoff and discovery, solution configuration, identity and integration setup, data validation, user acceptance testing, administrator enablement, and production readiness review. Work may overlap when dependencies allow.

## Customer responsibilities

The customer provides an executive sponsor, project manager, identity-provider administrator, integration owners, security contacts, and representative test users. The customer also supplies approved source data, test scenarios, timely decisions, and access to systems that are part of the agreed scope.

SAML configuration and data-mapping workshops occur during discovery. Delayed access, incomplete data, unavailable customer owners, or material scope changes can move the schedule.

## Timeline qualifications

Complex custom integrations, nonstandard migration requirements, or additional environments can change the timeline and require a separately approved implementation plan. The typical six-to-eight-week range must not be converted into a contractual deadline without delivery review and human approval.
