## Supported residency regions

Northstar offers customer-data residency in the United States and the European Union for Enterprise Cloud. The residency region is selected during ordering and provisioning. Standard Cloud is hosted in the United States and does not offer customer-selected European Union residency.

Residency describes the primary storage location for customer content and production backups. It does not guarantee that every operational activity, support interaction, or customer-controlled export remains inside the selected region.

## Data handling

Production access is limited to authorized personnel with a documented operational need. Administrative access requires strong authentication and is logged. Customers remain responsible for deciding what information they submit, configuring authorized users, and controlling files exported from the service.

Support personnel may process limited diagnostic information from approved support locations when necessary to resolve a case. A request for an absolute no-cross-border-access commitment requires security and legal review.

## Change and exception handling

A residency region cannot be changed by proposal language alone. Migration between regions requires technical assessment, an approved migration plan, and customer coordination. Countries, sovereign-cloud environments, or localization commitments not explicitly listed here are unsupported by current approved evidence and require human review.
