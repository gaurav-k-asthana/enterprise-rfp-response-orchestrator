## Active subscription

Customer content is retained while the subscription is active unless the customer deletes it through supported product functions or an approved legal hold applies. Backup copies follow the platform backup lifecycle and are not intended as a customer archive.

## Contract termination

Following contract termination, Northstar retains customer content for 30 calendar days to support an approved export or restoration request. After that period, production customer content is scheduled for deletion. Residual backup copies expire through the normal backup rotation.

## Commitment boundary

The standard post-termination retention period is 30 calendar days. A different customer-specific retention or deletion promise requires security, operations, and legal approval. This source does not authorize immediate deletion, indefinite retention, or a custom contractual deadline.

## Evaluation fixture notice

Another current, equal-authority synthetic source intentionally contains an incompatible post-termination period. Both values must remain visible during retrieval so the orchestrator can detect a direct conflict and request human review.
