## Standard availability target

Northstar's standard monthly service-availability target for production Enterprise Cloud is 99.9%. Standard Cloud uses the same operational target. Availability is measured at the service boundary using Northstar monitoring.

## Measurement exclusions

The standard calculation excludes announced maintenance, emergency maintenance, customer-controlled systems, unsupported configurations, customer network failures, force-majeure events, and suspension permitted by the agreement. This source describes an operational target and does not independently create a contractual remedy.

## Service credits and custom commitments

Service credits, measurement language, claim procedures, and contractual remedies are governed by an approved agreement. A request for 99.99%, a new service-credit schedule, or different exclusions is a nonstandard commitment and requires commercial, operations, and legal approval under the Proposal Commitment Authority Matrix.

## Response guidance

A proposal may accurately describe the 99.9% standard target with its qualifications. It must not state that a requested 99.99% SLA or service-credit term is accepted unless the required human approval has been recorded.
