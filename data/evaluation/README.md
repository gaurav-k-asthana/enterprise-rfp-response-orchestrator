This directory holds reviewed evaluation inputs and gold labels. It is separate from kb/ because evaluation answers must never become retrievable evidence.

evaluation_cases_v1.json contains the 24 canonical RFP inputs and the complete **FROZEN** V1 gold set. Gaurav Asthana approved all 24 cases in Step 4.7 after reviewing coverage, initial routing, evidence and source authority, atomic requirements, material claims, support, risk, HITL behavior, allowed human outcomes, allowed final statuses, primary failure hazards, and case rationales:

- routing_matrix_v1.md explains expected domains, the initial strategy family, selected peer specialists, and every routing reason.
- evidence_matrix_v1.md explains direct answer evidence and current/archived authority ordering, including tied conflicting sources and deliberately empty evidence sets.
